Hi again! I’m a Computer Science and German double major in my fourth year at Williams College.

I came to UX from robotics research. I spent two summers working on haptic feedback– essentially figuring out how to convey what a robot is touching to the person operating it. I absolutely loved the problem-solving side of research; problems were often so niche that solutions had to be innovated from scratch. But the questions I cared about most were always: how and why is someone going to use this? And how can we improve their experience?

In simplest terms, I love building things: from woodworking and knitting to pottery, 3D modeling, and robot systems (you'll find some of these projects on my art page). UX combines this hands-on, creative side with my technical background in computer science and research.My time in robotics taught me to think creatively within technical constraints, and I carry that mindset with me as a designer.

Language has shaped how I think about design as well. As a German major, French speaker, and lover of linguistics, I've always viewed concise, clear communication as a necessity. Presenting robotics research to general audiences and working across language barriers taught me that complex ideas are only useful once they're translated into something clear and natural to use. In my design philosophy, a good design is clear communication, and a good designer explain the reasoning behind every choice.

Outside of design, computer science, and writing portfolio "about me" sections, you might find me playing clarinet, ultimate frisbee, 3D modeling and animating, woodworking, or knitting (as you may have already guessed from my home page).
