Hi again! I'm a computer science and german double major in my fourth year at Williams College.

The concept of language has always fascinated me (as can be seen from the home page of this website)—a puzzle of working within constraints (e.g. grammar, syntax) to figure out how to most effectively and clearly communicate. For me, UX design is another kind of communication—a way to translate complex ideas into experiences that feel natural. As a computer science major, I am always mindful of technical limitations. My hope as a researcher and designer as that users don't have to be.

There never seems to be enough time to explore everything I want, but in my free time, you might find me playing clarinet, ultimate frisbee, 3d modelling/animating, or knitting.
