About Me

I'm a UX designer passionate about creating meaningful digital experiences that solve real problems. With a background in psychology and visual design, I bring both analytical thinking and creative vision to every project.

My design philosophy centers on empathy and iteration. I believe the best solutions emerge when we truly understand our users' needs, test our assumptions, and continuously refine our approach. Whether I'm conducting user research, sketching wireframes, or prototyping interactions, I'm always focused on the human at the other end of the screen.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring local coffee shops, experimenting with film photography, or learning about the latest design trends and technologies.
